# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme Project Management Documentation hub! This resource serves as your central entry point for understanding how we manage projects, coordinate teams, and deliver value to our customers. Whether you're joining the team, starting a new project, or looking to refine your understanding of our processes, you'll find the guidance you need here.

## Overview

At OctoAcme, we follow an **iterative, customer-centric approach** to project management that emphasizes delivering value early and often. Our process is built on five core principles: customer-first thinking, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety. These principles guide every project from conception through completion, ensuring that we build the right solutions while fostering a collaborative and transparent environment.

Our project lifecycle consists of **five distinct phases**: Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Retrospective. During Initiation, we validate the business need and align stakeholders around a clear problem statement. Planning involves detailed scoping, resource allocation, and milestone definition. Execution is where we build, test, and iterate on solutions through structured sprints with embedded quality assurance. The Release phase ensures smooth deployment and verification, followed by a Retrospective where we capture learnings and identify continuous improvement opportunities. Each phase is supported by key artifacts such as project charters, roadmaps, risk registers, and retrospective notes that maintain transparency and alignment across the organization.

**Roles and communication** are fundamental to our success. Our core team structure includes Project Managers who coordinate delivery and manage risks, Product Managers who define outcomes and prioritize work, Developers who implement features with a focus on quality, and QA/Testing professionals who validate acceptance criteria. We maintain regular communication rhythms including weekly syncs between PM and Product leads, daily or twice-weekly standups for delivery teams, and monthly stakeholder updates. This cadence ensures everyone stays informed while minimizing communication overhead.

**Quality assurance and risk management** are embedded throughout our process rather than treated as afterthoughts. We maintain comprehensive test coverage including unit, integration, and end-to-end tests, with clear definitions of done for each deliverable. Risk management is proactive and structured, utilizing risk registers that track impact, likelihood, ownership, and mitigation strategies. Our three-level escalation path (team-level → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor) ensures that issues are addressed promptly at the appropriate level. After each sprint, release, or incident, we conduct retrospectives to identify improvements and track action items, creating a culture of continuous learning and adaptation.

## Getting Started

If you're new to OctoAcme or starting a new project:

1. Start with the **[Project Management Overview](octoacme-project-management-overview.md)** to understand our core principles and approach
2. Review **[Roles and Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md)** to understand team structure and responsibilities
3. When ready to start a project, follow the **[Project Initiation](octoacme-project-initiation.md)** guide
4. Use the **[Project Kickoff Checklist](templates/project-kickoff-checklist.md)** to ensure comprehensive kickoff preparation
5. Develop your **[Stakeholder Communication Plan](templates/stakeholder-communication-checklist.md)** early in the project
6. Progress through the lifecycle phases as your project advances
7. Consult the **[Release Readiness Checklist](templates/release-readiness-checklist.md)** before any deployment
8. Use the **[Risk Review and Escalation Protocol](templates/risk-review-escalation-checklist.md)** throughout the project

## Quick Reference: Template Application by Role

Different roles will find different templates most relevant to their work:

- **Project Managers**: All templates, especially Project Kickoff, Stakeholder Communication Planning, and Risk Review
- **Product Managers**: Stakeholder Communication Planning, Project Kickoff (for alignment on vision and metrics)
- **Release Managers**: Release Readiness Checklist (primary responsibility)
- **QA Leads**: Release Readiness Checklist (testing sections), Project Kickoff (quality strategy)
- **DevOps Engineers**: Release Readiness Checklist (environment and deployment sections)
- **Developers**: Release Readiness Checklist (code and build sections), Project Kickoff (technical approach)
- **Business Analysts**: Stakeholder Communication Planning, Project Kickoff (requirements clarification)
- **UX Designers**: Project Kickoff (design approach), Stakeholder Communication (design feedback)
- **Technical Writers**: Release Readiness Checklist (documentation sections), Project Kickoff (documentation planning)

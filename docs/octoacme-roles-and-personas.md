# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers create user-facing designs, prototypes, and visual assets to ensure intuitive and accessible user experiences. They collaborate with Product Managers and Developers to balance usability with technical feasibility.

### Responsibilities
- Design user flows, wireframes, and interactive prototypes
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create visual design specifications and design system components
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met
- Validate designs with stakeholders and iterate based on feedback

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and improve satisfaction metrics
- Maintain design consistency across the product

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Manager and stakeholders
- Collaboration sessions with Developers during implementation
- User research findings and usability reports
- Design handoff documentation and specifications

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **With Product Manager**: Align on user needs, feature priorities, and success metrics
- **With Developers**: Ensure technical feasibility and smooth implementation of designs
- **With QA Lead**: Validate that implemented features match design specifications
- **With Technical Writer**: Coordinate on user-facing documentation and help content

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers oversee the release planning, coordination, and communication process. They ensure that releases are well-coordinated, properly documented, and communicated to all stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and maintain the release calendar
- Coordinate release activities across teams and dependencies
- Manage release documentation and communication
- Ensure compliance with release standards and policies
- Track release metrics and post-release health

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, on-time releases
- Minimize release-related incidents and rollbacks
- Maintain clear communication with all stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with Product and Engineering teams
- Release notes and stakeholder notifications
- Go/no-go decision meetings before deployment
- Post-release summaries and metrics reports

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **With Project Manager**: Align on release timelines and dependencies
- **With Developers**: Coordinate code freeze, branching, and deployment readiness
- **With QA Lead**: Verify testing completion and sign-off before release
- **With DevOps Engineer**: Coordinate deployment execution and monitoring
- **With Technical Writer**: Ensure release documentation is complete and accurate

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts gather detailed requirements from business units and translate them into technical specifications. They bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document detailed business requirements
- Analyze current processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Create functional specifications and user stories
- Facilitate requirements workshops and stakeholder meetings
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business needs

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and testable
- Improve alignment between business needs and technical solutions
- Reduce rework caused by unclear requirements

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering sessions with business stakeholders
- User story refinement with Product Manager and Developers
- Process flow diagrams and functional specifications
- Acceptance criteria validation

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **With Product Manager**: Refine product vision into detailed requirements
- **With Stakeholders**: Gather needs and validate proposed solutions
- **With Developers**: Clarify requirements and answer implementation questions
- **With QA Lead**: Define test scenarios based on business requirements

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create and maintain documentation that helps users and team members understand and use the product effectively. They work closely with technical teams to capture knowledge and make it accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Write user guides, API documentation, and help content
- Maintain internal process documentation and runbooks
- Review and edit technical content for clarity and accuracy
- Create documentation templates and style guidelines
- Ensure documentation is kept up-to-date with product changes

### Goals
- Provide clear, accurate, and accessible documentation
- Reduce support burden through self-service resources
- Maintain consistent documentation standards

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews with subject matter experts
- User feedback analysis to improve documentation
- Documentation plans and content inventories
- Style guide updates and writing workshops

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **With Developers**: Gather technical details and validate accuracy
- **With Product Manager**: Understand feature goals and user needs
- **With UX Designer**: Coordinate on in-product help and tooltips
- **With Release Manager**: Align on release documentation timing

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and oversee the quality assurance strategy for projects. They coordinate testing activities, manage defect triage, and serve as quality advocates throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy and test approach for projects
- Create and maintain test plans and test cases
- Coordinate testing activities across team members
- Track and prioritize defects and quality metrics
- Advocate for quality standards in planning and execution

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables that meet acceptance criteria
- Prevent defects through early testing and quality practices
- Maintain efficient and effective testing processes

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews with Project Manager and team
- Daily testing status and defect reports
- Quality gate decisions before releases
- Retrospective feedback on quality process improvements

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **With Developers**: Clarify acceptance criteria and review test coverage
- **With Product Manager**: Understand feature priorities and success criteria
- **With Release Manager**: Provide testing sign-off for releases
- **With Business Analyst**: Define test scenarios based on requirements

---

## Stakeholder (Customer Advocate)

### Role Summary
Stakeholders represent user needs and business objectives throughout the project lifecycle. They participate in key review points to ensure the product meets customer expectations and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Provide input on user needs and business priorities
- Review and approve key deliverables and milestones
- Participate in demos, planning sessions, and retrospectives
- Advocate for customer experience and business value
- Communicate project updates to broader stakeholder groups

### Goals
- Ensure the product delivers value to end users
- Maintain alignment between project work and business objectives
- Provide timely feedback to guide project decisions

### Typical Communication
- Sprint demos and milestone reviews
- Feedback on prototypes and early releases
- Stakeholder status updates and reports
- Requirements validation sessions

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **With Product Manager**: Define priorities and success metrics
- **With Project Manager**: Review project status and provide approvals
- **With UX Designer**: Validate designs meet user needs
- **With Business Analyst**: Clarify business requirements and processes

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers manage build, deployment, and monitoring infrastructure. They enable reliable, automated delivery pipelines and ensure operational readiness of systems.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure as code and environment provisioning
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting solutions
- Automate deployment and operational processes
- Advise on system architecture and scalability

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable, and repeatable deployments
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce manual operational overhead through automation

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure and deployment planning sessions
- Incident response and postmortem reviews
- Operational readiness reviews before releases
- System health and performance reports

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **With Developers**: Integrate code into CI/CD pipelines and troubleshoot deployment issues
- **With Release Manager**: Execute deployments and verify system health
- **With Project Manager**: Advise on infrastructure and operational risks
- **With QA Lead**: Provide test environments and deployment automation
